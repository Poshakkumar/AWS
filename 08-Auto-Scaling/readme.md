# 🚀 What is Auto Scaling?

Amazon EC2 Auto Scaling automatically launches new EC2 instances when demand increases and terminates unused instances when demand decreases.

This ensures that your application always has the right amount of compute resources.

---

# ❓ Why Auto Scaling?

Without Auto Scaling

- Manual scaling
- Downtime during traffic spikes
- Higher infrastructure costs
- Resource wastage

With Auto Scaling

- Automatic scaling
- High availability
- Better performance
- Cost optimization
